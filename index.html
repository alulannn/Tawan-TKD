About Us

Vientiane's home of
Taekwondo

Tawan Taekwondo Club was founded on 12 June 2010 by Southida Phommavong with a simple belief: that Taekwondo is not only a sport, but a path to building better people. Based at the Budo Centre, Vientiane, the club has grown over 15 years into a home for more than 100 active students of all ages and skill levels.

From first-time beginners stepping onto the mat to competitive athletes representing Laos at national level — every student trains in an environment that values discipline, respect, and continuous improvement. We are proud to have participated in the Lao Taekwondo Federation Championship and continue to build the next generation of Lao martial artists.

What Is Taekwondo

More than kicks.
A complete discipline.

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art and Olympic sport known for its emphasis on head-height kicks, fast kicking combinations, and jumping and spinning kicks. It develops the full person — body, mind, and character — through structured training, progressive belt ranks, and both individual and team competition.

Disciplines We Teach
01  ·  Sparring
Kyorugi (Olympic)
Full-contact sparring following World Taekwondo rules — the format used in the Olympic Games. Trains speed, timing, footwork, and strategic thinking under pressure.
02  ·  Forms
Poomsae
A sequence of offensive and defensive techniques performed in a set pattern. Develops precision, balance, breath control, and the deepest understanding of Taekwondo movement.
03  ·  Practical
Self-Defense
Applied Taekwondo techniques in realistic scenarios. Builds situational awareness, practical response skills, and the confidence to move safely in the world.
Why Train With Us
Mobility & Flexibility
Dynamic kicking and stretching routines progressively extend your range of motion and keep joints healthy for life.
Sharper Cognitive Function
Pattern memorisation, split-second reaction training, and strategic sparring sharpen focus, memory, and mental clarity.
Strength & Power
Full-body conditioning through kicking combinations, core exercises, and resistance training builds functional strength in every session.
Cardiovascular Fitness
High-intensity interval training within each session significantly improves heart health, endurance, and overall fitness levels.
